# Quality gate

## Pass/fail questions

### Story
- Can a viewer summarize the product value after one watch?
- Is there a clear hero moment?

### Product
- Is the product itself visible and truthful?
- Are the most useful real UI states used?

### Design
- Does the color/type/composition system feel like one art-directed piece?
- Are there fewer visual ingredients than there are effects?

### Motion
- Does movement communicate hierarchy?
- Are there real holds?
- Do scene transitions preserve spatial/semantic continuity?

### Technical
- Are there frame drops, clipping, unreadable text, jitter, or accidental collisions?
- Does the final export have the intended dimensions, frame rate, and audio?

### Anti-AI test
Ask:

> “Which 5 seconds would make a human motion designer immediately recognize this as generic AI output?”

Fix those seconds first.
